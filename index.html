3. Iconic Performances

He revolutionized live performance with the moonwalk debut in 1983 (“Billie Jean”) and massive global events like the Super Bowl XXVII halftime show.

4. Media & Conspiracy Theories

Due to extreme fame and media attention, many rumors and conspiracy theories formed around his appearance, lifestyle, and personal life.

5. Impact & Importance

Michael Jackson reshaped global pop culture through music, dance, fashion, and music video storytelling. His influence is still seen in modern artists worldwide.

6. Change Over Time

He evolved from child star → global pop icon → controversial media figure → lasting cultural legend. Key turning points: Thriller (1982), Bad (1987), Dangerous (1991).
