Origins

Michael Jackson grew up in Gary, Indiana under extreme structural pressure: - working-class economic conditions - strict father-driven discipline system - Motown industrial music control By age 11, he was lead singer of the Jackson 5 due to vocal dominance and emotional performance ability. This created a childhood shaped by professional expectation rather than normal development.
