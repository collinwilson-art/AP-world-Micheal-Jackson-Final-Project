2. Origins (Jackson 5)

Michael rose to fame in The Jackson 5, becoming lead vocalist due to unmatched talent and performance ability.

3. Iconic Performances

Moonwalk debut (1983), Super Bowl XXVII halftime show, and global stadium tours transformed live entertainment forever.

4. Conspiracy Theories

Extreme fame and media attention led to speculation and misinformation about his appearance and life.

5. Impact

Michael Jackson reshaped global culture through music, dance, fashion, and media innovation.

6. Change Over Time

Child star → global icon → controversial figure → permanent cultural legacy.
