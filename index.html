History & Rise

Michael Jackson (1958–2009) rose from The Jackson 5 to become one of the most influential entertainers of all time. He revolutionized music videos, live performance, and global pop culture.

The Jackson 5

The Jackson 5 launched Michael’s career at Motown.
  • I Want You Back
  • ABC
  • I'll Be There
  • The Love You Save
Michael quickly became the breakout star due to his voice and stage presence.

Super Bowl Halftime Show (1993)

Michael Jackson transformed the Super Bowl halftime show into a global entertainment event. His performance was watched by over 130 million people.

Moonwalk

Michael popularized the moonwalk in 1983 during Motown 25: Yesterday, Today, Forever. It became one of the most iconic dance moves in history.

Pepsi Accident (1984)

During a Pepsi commercial shoot, pyrotechnics accidentally set Michael’s hair on fire. He suffered second-degree burns and lifelong scalp damage.

Vitiligo & Health

Michael Jackson had vitiligo, a skin condition causing loss of skin pigmentation. He also suffered chronic pain from injuries throughout his career.

Controversy & Public Life

Allegations

Michael faced child abuse allegations in the 1990s and 2000s and was acquitted in 2005.

Macaulay Culkin

Macaulay Culkin publicly stated Michael Jackson never abused him and defended their friendship.

Media Pressure

His life was heavily scrutinized by global media throughout his career.

Death & Conspiracy Theories

Michael Jackson died in 2009 from acute propofol intoxication. Some conspiracy theories claim he faked his death, but there is no evidence supporting this claim.
