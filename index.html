2. Origins & The Jackson 5

Early Life

Michael Jackson was born in Gary, Indiana in 1958 and began performing at age 5.

Joe Jackson & Pressure

His father Joe Jackson was extremely strict, controlling rehearsals and enforcing intense discipline, which created a difficult childhood relationship filled with pressure and perfectionism.

Jackson 5 Rise

The Jackson 5 signed with Motown in 1969 when Michael was 11 years old. He became the lead singer and global breakout star.

Hit Songs

  • I Want You Back
  • ABC
  • The Love You Save
  • I'll Be There
  • Dancing Machine

Impact

Their rapid global success brought fame, touring pressure, and shaped Michael’s perfectionist work ethic.

3. Famous Performances & Tours

Super Bowl XXVII (1993 - Rose Bowl, Pasadena)

Michael Jackson was 34 years old. He performed at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena, California. It became the most influential halftime show in history.

  • Jam intro
  • Billie Jean (moonwalk)
  • Black or White
  • Heal the World

Motown 25 (1983)

At age 25, Michael performed Billie Jean and introduced the moonwalk to the world.

Bad World Tour (1987–1989)

His first solo tour with over 4 million attendees worldwide.

Dangerous Tour (1992–1993)

Massive stadium tour with advanced stage effects and extreme physical demand.

HIStory Tour (1996–1997)

Largest tour of his career with over 4.5 million attendees.

Impact of Touring

Tours made him a global icon but also caused exhaustion, stress, and lifelong pressure.
