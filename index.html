Origins

Michael Jackson grew up in Gary, Indiana under strict discipline and industrial music pressure. Key forces: - Joseph Jackson’s strict training system - Motown structured performance system - early exposure to gospel + R&B Result: extreme performance precision from childhood.

Jackson 5

Michael became lead singer at ~11 due to vocal dominance. Songs: - I Want You Back - ABC - The Love You Save - I’ll Be There Represents early industrial child stardom.
