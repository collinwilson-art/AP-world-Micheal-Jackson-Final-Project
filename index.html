Michael Jackson: The King of Pop

Albums • History • Performances • Cultural Impact

1. Albums (Chronological Order)

Got to Be There (1972)

Background: Michael’s debut solo album while still in The Jackson 5.

Reason: Motown launched him as a solo child prodigy.

  • Got to Be There
  • Rockin’ Robin
  • Ben
Ben (1972)

Background: Emotional early solo work tied to the film "Ben".

Reason: Show emotional depth as a young artist.

  • Ben
  • Greatest Show on Earth
  • People Make the World Go Round
Off the Wall (1979)

Background: Break from Motown sound with Quincy Jones.

Reason: Establish adult artistic identity.

  • Don’t Stop ’Til You Get Enough
  • Rock With You
  • She’s Out of My Life
Thriller (1982)

Background: Global crossover pop masterpiece.

Reason: Create a perfect no-skip album.

  • Thriller
  • Billie Jean
  • Beat It
Bad (1987)

Background: First fully self-controlled project.

Reason: Prove dominance after Thriller.

  • Bad
  • Smooth Criminal
  • Man in the Mirror
Dangerous (1991)

Background: New Jack Swing era innovation.

Reason: Reinvent sound for new decade.

  • Black or White
  • Remember the Time
HIStory (1995)

Background: Response to media pressure.

Reason: Defend legacy and express emotion.

  • Earth Song
  • Scream
  • They Don’t Care About Us
Invincible (2001)

Background: Final major studio album.

Reason: Modern R&B comeback attempt.

  • Butterflies
  • You Rock My World
  • Break of Dawn
Xscape (2014)

Background: Posthumous release of unreleased material.

Reason: Modern production of archived songs.

  • Xscape
  • Love Never Felt So Good

2. Origins (Jackson 5)

Started with The Jackson 5, becoming lead vocalist due to exceptional talent and stage presence.

3. Iconic Performances

Moonwalk debut (1983), Super Bowl XXVII, and global stadium tours changed live entertainment forever.

4. Media & Conspiracy Theories

Fame led to intense speculation and media-driven narratives around his life.

5. Impact

He reshaped global music, dance, fashion, and modern entertainment industries.

6. Change Over Time

Child star → global icon → controversial figure → permanent cultural legend.
