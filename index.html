Michael Jackson

History of Michael Jackson

Michael Jackson was born on August 29, 1958, in Gary, Indiana. He began performing at a very young age alongside his brothers in The Jackson 5. His father, Joe Jackson, pushed the group to practice constantly, which helped them become one of the biggest musical groups in the world.


Michael quickly became the standout star because of his powerful voice, incredible dancing ability, and stage presence. As he grew older, he transitioned into a solo artist and eventually became the most famous entertainer on Earth during the 1980s and 1990s.


Michael Jackson revolutionized music videos, dancing, live performances, fashion, and pop culture. He became known worldwide as "The King of Pop."

The Jackson 5 Success

The Jackson 5 became one of the most successful musical groups of the late 1960s and early 1970s. The group consisted of Michael and his brothers Jackie, Tito, Jermaine, and Marlon.


After signing with Motown Records, they exploded in popularity with hit songs like:

  • I Want You Back
  • ABC
  • The Love You Save
  • I'll Be There

Michael amazed audiences because of his mature singing voice and confidence despite being so young. The Jackson 5 helped break racial barriers in pop music and became one of the first Black groups to achieve massive global success.

The Moonwalk

Michael Jackson made the moonwalk famous during his 1983 performance of Billie Jean on Motown 25. Although versions of the move existed before him, Michael perfected it and turned it into one of the most iconic dance moves ever created.


The audience was shocked when Michael appeared to glide backwards across the stage. The move instantly became legendary and is still copied by dancers around the world today.


The moonwalk became one of the defining symbols of Michael Jackson's career and helped separate him from every other entertainer of his generation.

1993 Super Bowl Halftime Show

Michael Jackson's 1993 Super Bowl halftime performance completely changed the future of halftime shows forever. Before Michael performed, halftime shows were much smaller and less important. After his performance, the Super Bowl became known for massive celebrity performances.


Michael stood completely still for nearly two minutes while the crowd screamed. The moment became one of the most iconic entrances in television history.


He performed Jam, Billie Jean, Black or White, We Are the World, and Heal the World. The performance attracted over 130 million viewers and even caused TV ratings to increase during halftime.


Many people consider it the greatest halftime show ever because it transformed the Super Bowl into a worldwide entertainment event.

Michael Jackson Albums

Off the Wall (1979)

This album transformed Michael Jackson from a child star into a mature solo artist. It mixed disco, funk, and R&B sounds and included hits like Don't Stop 'Til You Get Enough and Rock with You.

Thriller (1982)

Thriller became the best-selling album in music history. Its music videos changed the entertainment industry forever and made Michael the most famous celebrity on Earth.

Bad (1987)

Bad proved Michael could continue dominating music after Thriller. The album produced five number-one singles and featured darker themes and stronger dance music.

Dangerous (1991)

Dangerous mixed pop music with hip-hop and new jack swing influences. It featured socially conscious songs like Black or White and Heal the World.

HIStory (1995)

HIStory reflected Michael's anger toward the media and public criticism. Many songs focused on fame, loneliness, betrayal, and racism.

Invincible (2001)

Invincible became Michael's final studio album released during his lifetime. It experimented with modern R&B and pop production styles.

Michael (2010)

Released after his death, this album used unfinished recordings Michael created before he died.

Xscape (2014)

Xscape featured unreleased Michael Jackson songs that producers modernized after his death to introduce his music to a new generation.

Michael Jackson's Death

Michael Jackson died from acute propofol intoxication while preparing for his "This Is It" comeback concerts in 2009.


His doctor, Conrad Murray, was convicted of involuntary manslaughter for improperly administering the medication.


After Michael's death, conspiracy theories spread online claiming he faked his death to escape fame, pressure, or financial struggles. No evidence has ever proven these theories.

Child Abuse Allegations & Controversies

Michael Jackson faced several accusations of child sexual abuse during the 1990s and 2000s. These accusations became one of the biggest controversies in entertainment history.


In 2005, Michael Jackson went to trial and was found not guilty on all criminal charges. Supporters argued he was unfairly targeted because of his fame and unusual behavior. Critics believed the accusations permanently damaged his reputation.


Actor Macaulay Culkin, one of Michael Jackson's closest celebrity friends, publicly stated multiple times that Michael Jackson never abused him. Culkin defended Michael in interviews and testified during the 2005 trial.


Vitiligo & Appearance Changes

Michael Jackson suffered from vitiligo, a skin condition that causes patches of skin to lose pigmentation. As the condition spread, his appearance changed dramatically over time.


Many people incorrectly believed he intentionally changed his skin color. Michael publicly explained that vitiligo caused his skin to become lighter and that he used makeup to even out his skin tone.


Pepsi Commercial Accident

In 1984, while filming a Pepsi commercial, pyrotechnics accidentally set Michael Jackson's hair on fire. He suffered second-degree burns and underwent painful medical treatment afterward.


Some people believe the accident and surgeries afterward contributed to his later struggles with pain medication.

Historical Thinking Skills

Cause & Effect

Michael Jackson's music videos, dancing, and performances changed global entertainment forever. MTV, celebrity culture, and live performances became much larger because of his influence.


Comparison

Michael Jackson can be compared to artists like Beyoncé and Taylor Swift because of their worldwide popularity and influence. However, Michael transformed music videos and dance performance on an even larger historical scale.


AP World Connection

Michael Jackson connects to globalization and cultural diffusion. His music spread worldwide through television, radio, concerts, and the internet.
