2. Origins (Jackson 5)

Michael rose to fame with The Jackson 5, becoming lead vocalist due to exceptional talent and stage presence.

3. Iconic Performances

Moonwalk debut (1983), Super Bowl XXVII, and global tours revolutionized live performance.

4. Conspiracy Theories

Extreme fame led to widespread media speculation and misinformation.

5. Impact

Michael Jackson reshaped global music, dance, fashion, and entertainment industries.

6. Change Over Time

Child star → global icon → controversial figure → cultural legend.
