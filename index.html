History

Michael Jackson (1958–2009) was a global entertainer known as the King of Pop. He transformed music videos, dance, and global pop culture forever.

Jackson 5

The Jackson 5 launched Michael’s career with Motown Records.
  • I Want You Back
  • ABC
  • I'll Be There
He became the standout member due to his vocal ability and stage presence.

Super Bowl Performance (1993)

Michael Jackson changed Super Bowl halftime shows forever. His 1993 performance became one of the most-watched events in TV history.

Moonwalk

Michael Jackson popularized the moonwalk in 1983 during Motown 25. It became one of the most iconic dance moves ever.

Controversy & Personal Life

Michael Jackson faced allegations, media scrutiny, and legal cases during his life.


He was found not guilty in 2005. Actor Macaulay Culkin stated he was never harmed by Jackson.


He also had vitiligo and suffered injuries in the Pepsi commercial accident.


He died in 2009 from acute propofol intoxication. Conspiracy theories followed but have no proof.
