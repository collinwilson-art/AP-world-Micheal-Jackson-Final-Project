2. Origins & The Jackson 5

Early Life

Michael Jackson was born in Gary, Indiana in 1958. He began performing at the age of 5, showing natural talent in singing and dancing.

Joe Jackson & Strict Upbringing

His father Joe Jackson was extremely strict, demanding perfection in rehearsals and performances. Michael has described his childhood as disciplined but emotionally difficult, with constant pressure to perform at a professional level.

The Jackson 5 Formation

The Jackson 5 formed in the 1960s and signed with Motown Records in 1969 when Michael was only 11 years old. He quickly became the lead singer due to his powerful voice and emotional stage presence.

Major Hits

  • I Want You Back
  • ABC
  • The Love You Save
  • I'll Be There
  • Dancing Machine

Impact

The Jackson 5 became global stars almost instantly, bringing Michael into international fame at a very young age. This early exposure created both massive opportunity and long-term pressure that shaped his perfectionism.

3. Famous Performances & Tours

Super Bowl XXVII (1993 - Rose Bowl, Pasadena)

Michael Jackson was 34 years old when he performed at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena, California. This performance is considered one of the most important live events in entertainment history.

  • Jam intro
  • Billie Jean (moonwalk performance)
  • Black or White
  • Heal the World

He stood motionless for over a minute before beginning, creating tension and anticipation that changed live performance standards forever.

Motown 25 (1983)

At age 25, Michael performed Billie Jean and introduced the moonwalk to a global audience. This moment defined his identity as a performer.

Major Tours

Bad World Tour (1987–1989): His first solo world tour, reaching over 4 million people worldwide.

Dangerous World Tour (1992–1993): Featured advanced stage effects and massive global stadiums, but created intense physical exhaustion.

HIStory World Tour (1996–1997): His largest tour ever, performing for over 4.5 million fans across the world.

Impact of Touring

These tours made Michael the biggest live performer on Earth, but also caused extreme physical and emotional strain due to constant travel, pressure, and perfectionism.
