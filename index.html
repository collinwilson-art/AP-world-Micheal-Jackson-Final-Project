History & Rise

Michael Jackson (1958–2009) was an American singer, dancer, and entertainer known as the King of Pop. He transformed global music, dance, fashion, and performance culture.

The Jackson 5

Michael began his career in The Jackson 5, one of the first Black American boy bands to achieve global success.
  • I Want You Back
  • ABC
  • I'll Be There
  • The Love You Save
He quickly became the breakout star due to his vocal ability and stage presence.

1993 Super Bowl Halftime Show

Michael Jackson’s performance revolutionized Super Bowl entertainment. It was watched by over 130 million people and introduced the modern halftime show format.

The Moonwalk

Michael Jackson popularized the moonwalk in 1983 during Motown 25. Although he did not invent it, he perfected it and made it globally iconic.

Pepsi Commercial Accident

In 1984, Michael suffered burns during a Pepsi commercial when pyrotechnics malfunctioned. This led to long-term scalp injuries and pain management issues.

Health (Vitiligo & Pain)

Michael Jackson had vitiligo, a skin condition that causes loss of pigmentation. He also suffered chronic pain from injuries throughout his career.

Controversy & Public Life

Child Abuse Allegations

Michael faced allegations in the 1990s and 2000s. He was acquitted in 2005.

Macaulay Culkin

Actor Macaulay Culkin publicly stated that Michael Jackson never abused him and defended their friendship.

Media Scrutiny

His life was heavily covered and often sensationalized by tabloids.

Death & Conspiracy Theories

Michael Jackson died in 2009 from acute propofol intoxication. Some conspiracy theories claim he faked his death, but there is no evidence supporting this.
