2. Origins (Jackson 5)

Michael rose to fame in The Jackson 5, becoming lead vocalist due to unmatched talent and stage presence.

3. Iconic Performances

Moonwalk debut (1983), Super Bowl XXVII, and global tours redefined live entertainment.

4. Conspiracy Theories

Extreme fame led to media speculation and misinformation about his life.

5. Impact

He reshaped global music, dance, and entertainment culture worldwide.

6. Change Over Time

Child star → global icon → controversial figure → cultural legend.
