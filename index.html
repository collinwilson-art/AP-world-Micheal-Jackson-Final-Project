History & Rise to Fame

Michael Jackson (1958–2009) began performing as a child in The Jackson 5 before becoming the most influential solo entertainer in history. He revolutionized pop music, music videos, live performance, and global celebrity culture.


He became known for unmatched stage presence, record-breaking albums, and iconic dance moves like the moonwalk.

The Jackson 5

The Jackson 5 were a Motown group that launched Michael Jackson’s career. They became global stars in the late 1960s and early 1970s.

  • I Want You Back
  • ABC
  • I'll Be There

Michael quickly became the standout performer due to his vocal talent and charisma.

1993 Super Bowl Halftime Show

Michael Jackson’s Super Bowl performance changed entertainment history. Before 1993, halftime shows were small. After him, they became global spectacles.


He performed for over 130 million viewers and stood motionless for nearly 2 minutes before starting — creating one of the most iconic entrances ever.

The Moonwalk

Michael Jackson popularized the moonwalk during his 1983 performance of Billie Jean. Although he did not invent it, he perfected it and made it globally famous.


It became one of the most iconic dance moves in history and a signature of his career.

Controversies & Public Life

Child Abuse Allegations

Michael Jackson faced accusations during the 1990s and 2000s. He was found not guilty in his 2005 trial.


Macaulay Culkin

Actor Macaulay Culkin stated multiple times that Michael Jackson never abused him and defended their friendship publicly.


Vitiligo

Michael Jackson had vitiligo, a skin condition that caused loss of pigmentation and changes in appearance.


Pepsi Accident

A 1984 Pepsi commercial accident caused burns to his scalp after pyrotechnics malfunctioned.


Death & Conspiracies

He died in 2009 from acute propofol intoxication. Conspiracy theories claim he faked his death, but there is no evidence.
